# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project built using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into employee demographics, workforce attrition, job satisfaction, education fields, and years of service.

The dashboard helps HR teams explore workforce patterns and monitor key HR metrics through interactive visualizations and KPI cards.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and the overall attrition rate.
- Understand employee distribution across age groups and genders.
- Explore attrition patterns by years at the company.
- Visualize employee distribution by education field.
- Compare job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Present key HR metrics in a single interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation, if used
- **DAX** — KPI calculations, if used
- **Data Visualization** — KPI cards, bar charts, area charts, donut charts, and stacked bar charts

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Overview
Displays total employees, attrition cases, attrition rate, average salary, average age, and average years at the company.

### 2. Attrition by Age Group
Visualizes employee attrition across age groups, including 18–25, 26–35, 36–45, 46–55, and 55+.

### 3. Attrition by Years at Company
Uses an area chart to explore how attrition cases are distributed across employees' years of service.

### 4. Attrition by Education Field
Uses a donut chart to show attrition distribution across education fields such as Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, Technical Degree, Other, and Human Resources.

### 5. Gender Distribution
Displays the workforce distribution:
- Female employees: 588
- Male employees: 882

### 6. Job Satisfaction Analysis
Uses a stacked bar chart to compare job satisfaction ratings across different job roles.

## 💡 Key Insights

- The dashboard reports **237 attrition cases out of 1,470 employees**, with an attrition rate of approximately **16.1%**.
- Male employees account for 882 employees, while female employees account for 588.
- The 26–35 age group has the highest count in the displayed age-group chart.
- Life Sciences is the largest category in the displayed education-field attrition breakdown.
- Job satisfaction ratings vary across the job roles shown in the dashboard.

*Note: These are descriptive findings from the dashboard. They do not establish the causes of employee attrition.*
